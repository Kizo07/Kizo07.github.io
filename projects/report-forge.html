Typography

Sell-side polish, programmatic.

  • Eight templates — standard, memo, whitepaper, modern, studio, portfolio-light, portfolio-dark, bespoke — each a real Typst layout, not a CSS skin.
  • Cover infographics on page one: KPI strip, conviction verdict band, key-point cards, and a fixed three-scenario strip, compacted so the body breaks cleanly to page two.
  • Spreadsheet treatment for showcase tables — banded headers, zebra rows, tabular numerals, right-aligned numbers — identical in HTML and PDF.

Discipline

Gates, not vibes.

  • Every figure must pass mechanical checks — resolution, aspect, legibility, provenance — or the build fails, and no blurry chart ships.
  • Native Exhibit numbering with cross-references, so "see Exhibit 12" survives reordering and regeneration.
  • Burned-in provenance: as-of dates and data stamps on every exhibit, carrying the research engine's audit discipline into print.

In production

The rendering half of QuantFlow.

  • Renders QuantFlow's flagship equity outlooks — the AMZN 24-page and MRNA 12-page reports share one pipeline, with different briefs.
  • One scaffold call per report: agents pick a template, place the charts, and the forge enforces the layout contract.
  • A gold/teal identity for exhibits across dark and light print themes — the reports and the site share one visual system.
