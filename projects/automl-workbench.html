AutoML Workbench

I led the development of a staff-gated workbench for training, comparing, explaining and scoring models without writing code. It gave researchers a way to evaluate signals on a built-in U.S. equity panel or train models on their own published tables.

Validation

Out-of-sample model comparison

  • For equity panels, purged walk-forward cross-validation removes training labels that overlap the test window. An embargo adds a gap, and a separate holdout spans multiple dates.
  • The equity leaderboard ranks supervised models by Rank IC IR, the mean cross-sectional Spearman rank correlation divided by its standard deviation across holdout dates.
  • Published tables use walk-forward splits when they have a time column and seeded K-fold otherwise, with grouping or stratification as appropriate. The table leaderboard uses R² for regression and balanced accuracy for classification.

Workbench

Training, explanation and scoring

  • Both data sources support regression, classification and clustering. The equity panel also supports dimensionality reduction and similarity search.
  • Quick Start trains seven fast models on the equity panel. Hyperparameter search runs within a trial budget, and supported boosting models use early stopping on a validation fold.
  • For supervised runs, researchers can inspect out-of-fold predictions and feature rankings, explain individual predictions with SHAP, and combine top models into an ensemble. Predict scores the latest or a historical equity snapshot, or queues a scoring job over a published table.

Engineering

Run execution and reproducible releases

  • The API validates a run before sending it to a background worker. The interface streams progress, and the engine stores the fitted model, configuration and results for later inspection and scoring.
  • Versioned specifications define the estimator catalog, defaults and validation rules. Oversized tables receive an explicit refusal, and published-table permissions are checked again when reading or exporting results.
  • Engine releases pin the source revision and verify the build artifact by checksum. The workbench uses the installed engine's capabilities to decide which controls to show.

Verification

What the staging tests established

Staging tests checked engineered feature values and independently recalculated metrics. They also tested repeatability, cancellation and concurrent runs. A deliberately leaky feature served as a positive control for the leakage check.

The staged equity panel filtered historical data by current listing status, which introduces survivorship bias into historical tests.
