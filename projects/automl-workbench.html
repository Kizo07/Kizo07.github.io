Validation

Scores that never see the future.

On financial panels, an honest score is the whole product, so leakage protection is built into every split, not left to the user.

  • Purged walk-forward cross-validation with an embargo, plus a multi-date holdout, for time-ordered data; seeded grouped or stratified K-fold for plain tables, chosen automatically from the data's roles.
  • Leakage protection proven, not assumed: a positive-control test plants a deliberately leaky column and confirms the pipeline catches it.
  • Panel leaderboards rank on holdout Rank IC IR (Spearman IC mean over its standard deviation across dates), the metric a quant desk actually trades on; plain tables rank on R² or balanced accuracy.

Workbench

From raw table to explained, deployable model with no code.

Benchmarked against a commercial AutoML product, then closed the highest-value gaps: SHAP drivers, blending, holdout scoring and Quick start.

  • Quick start trains seven fast models in one click; hyperparameter search runs under a budget with early stopping; past-only lags, changes and rolling features plus sector/size neutralization are built in.
  • Every run ships a report, model tear sheet, feature importance, per-row SHAP prediction drivers, "Blend top N" ensembles and out-of-fold predictions, all exportable to sheets or a zip bundle.
  • Predict scores the latest or an as-of snapshot, or queues scoring of every row; live progress streams over SSE, and the same engine is callable from notebooks and the in-product AI agent.

Production

Built to refuse loudly, never fail silently.

Shipped in production releases behind a staff feature flag, with the engineering discipline of a platform, not a prototype.

  • Spec-driven engine: algorithms, limits, defaults and user-facing wording live in versioned JSON specs (one consolidation removed ~20K lines), and 31 plain-language error codes keep jargon away from users.
  • Set the policy, applied across 438 data limits, that every cap is a named refusal and never a silent cut; a Redis/RQ worker pool refuses work rather than losing it, and per-dataset access rights are re-checked on every read and export.
  • Cut four reproducible engine releases (pinned commit, wheel sha256) with additive, backward-compatible API contracts across five codebases, validated by a 5-tester staging pass and a 31-item divergence audit that unified two workflows into one.
