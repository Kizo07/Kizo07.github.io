Quantlink AI | Factor Research

Single-factor research as a complete decision workflow.

Built as the flagship Quantlink AI modeling page: users can choose a metric, lookback window, and bucket scheme, then watch the entire analytical stack recompute from distribution to signal quality to risk-adjusted backtest.

Research Frame

What the page answers

  • What does the factor distribution look like across the full 3,545-stock universe?
  • Where is the factor concentrated by sector, industry, and market-cap band?
  • Does the factor rank stocks in a way that predicts forward 12-month returns?
  • Do long-short or top-minus-bottom buckets survive risk and benchmark comparison?
Build

Dashboard architecture

The dashboard is structured like a professional factor researcher thinks: first understand the cross-section, then inspect concentration risk, then test predictive power, then evaluate performance and drawdown behavior.

Product Decision

The key design choice

The page does not stop at a single performance chart. It forces the factor to explain itself through distribution, concentration, predictive power, signal quality, realized performance, and risk. That makes the output more useful for non-quant users without flattening the analytics.

  • Controls recompute all panels on one state change.
  • The key-takeaway panel summarizes backtest highlights, sector bias, and signal quality in one glanceable read.
  • Conditional formatting and reference overlays keep dense outputs scannable.
