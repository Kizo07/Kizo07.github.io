Quantitative equity research at Wolfe

At Wolfe Research, I built equity factor models, used natural language processing to analyze company disclosures, and developed tools for measuring portfolio exposure to market shocks.

RoleSenior Quantitative Research Associate
TeamQuantitative Research, ESG and Strategy (QES)
DatesDec 2020–Jul 2024

A factor model for loss-making companies

I combined fundamental, technical, and alternative signals in a risk-neutralized factor model using an ensemble of XGBoost and Elastic Net. I analyzed what drove factor performance and used an event study to measure how markets reacted to corporate turnarounds.

Intangible assets in a multifactor risk model

I adjusted book-to-market to account for intangible assets and incorporated the measure into multifactor risk models for portfolio optimization. I also examined the relationship between intangible assets and information asymmetry.

Financial NLP for earnings calls and filings

I built an NLP system that analyzed earnings-call transcripts and audio, 10-K and 10-Q filings, and news. I used transformer models including RoBERTa, Llama, and GPT, alongside knowledge graphs, to score sentiment, model topics, and recognize entities.

Equity exposures to macro shocks

I built cross-sectional exposure screens and scenario analyses to show which equities and portfolios were sensitive to shocks involving interest rates, oil, geopolitics, or technology.

Dashboards for backtesting and stock screening

I led the design of dashboards for researchers to run backtests, screen stocks dynamically, and evaluate trading strategies.
