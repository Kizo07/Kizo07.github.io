Options, Rates, and Securitized Products

Numerical pricing methods for instruments where closed form is not enough.

A research project covering American and barrier options, mortgage-backed securities, Monte Carlo variance reduction, finite-difference Greeks, and G2++ interest-rate models.

Back to Projects Open GitHub
Core Methods

Pricing toolkit

  • Binomial and trinomial lattices for early-exercise features and path-dependent logic.
  • Least Squares Monte Carlo for American option exercise policies.
  • Finite difference methods for price surfaces and Greeks.
  • Monte Carlo with antithetic and control variates to reduce estimator variance.
  • G2++ interest-rate modeling for curve-sensitive fixed-income instruments.
Why it matters

From derivative math to securitized risk

Securitized products combine embedded optionality, prepayment behavior, rate sensitivity, and cash-flow timing. The project treats numerical methods as a common language for valuing those nonlinear risks rather than as separate textbook techniques.
