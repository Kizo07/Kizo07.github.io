Thesis

Evidence-first, adversarial, printable

Most agent research demos stop at a chat transcript. QuantFlow ends at a deliverable a desk would sign: sourced numbers, debated picks, sized positions, rendered documents.

  • Point-in-time S&P 500 membership back to 1996 — historical selections never touch the current snapshot, so delisted names can't silently vanish.
  • A fixed desk workflow: parallel evidence tracks, an independent bull/bear debate with documented demotions, and a risk-manager veto before any recommendation.
  • Length, freshness, and artifact gates are mechanical — word counts, as-of dates, and rendered-file checks, not judgment calls.
