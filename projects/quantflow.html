QuantFlow

QuantFlow uses historical S&P 500 membership to research individual stocks. Eleven specialist agents collect evidence, record a bull and bear debate, and run a risk review that can veto a recommendation before generating a report with charts.

How the research works

  • The data engine uses S&P 500 membership as of each research date, back to 1996. That keeps stocks that later delisted in the historical universe.
  • Specialists gather evidence in parallel. The bull and bear cases are evaluated independently, with downgrades documented and a risk manager able to veto the recommendation.
  • Automated checks verify word counts, as-of dates, and rendered files before delivery.
