Pipeline

Every step, one CLI.

  • Extraction, training-data builds, LLM-assisted labeling, fine-tuning, inference, embeddings, and factor construction — each a subcommand with checkpoints, orchestrated by run-all.
  • Hawk/dove lexicon baseline and per-speaker profiles anchor the model-based scores.
  • Factor series regress against SPX returns under walk-forward evaluation — no in-sample shortcuts.

Dashboard

Read the meetings, not just the scores.

  • Overview — sentiment, labor, inflation, and novelty time series across meetings.
  • Meeting explorer — every paragraph with its labels and confidence; hawkish red, dovish green.
  • Novelty explorer — per-meeting and per-paragraph novelty against the previous meeting, from embeddings.
  • Speakers — hawk/dove profiles and per-meeting trends.
