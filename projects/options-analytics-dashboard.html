Modules

Five modules share one pricing core.

Every module draws on the same vendored comp_methods library, so the price in the builder matches the price in the Greeks lab.

ModuleWhat it does
Strategy BuilderUp to 6 legs of calls, puts, and stock — buy or sell, European or American — with payoff and current-value overlays plus portfolio Greeks.
American OptionsFive pricing models side by side with early-exercise premiums vs. Black–Scholes, runtimes, and convergence charts.
Greeks LabTwo instruments on one chart: Δ, Γ, ν, Θ, ρ against spot, time, vol, and rates — 20 charts plus N(d1)/N(d2) moneyness.
Implied VolatilityEuropean (Brent root-find) and American (trinomial) IV with back-solve verification and a live-quote workflow.
ExoticsAsian, barrier, and lookback via seeded Monte Carlo with standard errors; digital options in closed form.

Design

Trading conventions baked in

The dashboard follows desk conventions, so its outputs need no mental conversion.

  • Theta per day; vega and rho per 1% move — displayed the way traders quote them.
  • Ticker → expiry → strike → bid/ask/mid auto-fill for real-quote IV work.
  • Dividend yield support and Yahoo Finance spot fetching keep the builder honest on real names.

Lineage

From pricing library to product

The numerical methods research became the backbone of the product.

  • comp_methods — trees, finite differences, LSM Monte Carlo, Greeks, and rate models — is vendored from the American_Options project.
  • v3 rebuilds and supersedes Options_Dashboard2, with a Mantine/React frontend migration underway.
