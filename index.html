01 · Current Role

Founding researcher at QuantLink AI

2026 — Present · Quant Analytics

Institutional factor research, made into product.

At QuantLink AI I own research and product work that brings institutional-grade factor modeling to a broader market of traders and students — a single workflow connecting data, diagnostics, backtests, and risk.

  • Point-in-time factor pipeline — end-to-end research infrastructure ingesting NASDAQ fundamentals and SEC XBRL facts, with ratios, Fama-French factors, and technical indicators assembled for cross-sectional analysis.
  • Signal diagnostics — Rank IC, information ratio, Pearson correlation, OLS slope, monotonicity, and top-minus-bottom spread, evaluated on a 3,545-stock universe.
  • Risk-aware evaluation — factors neutralized against Fama-French and sector exposures; performance decomposed across market cap and industry.
  • Backtest engine — quintile and decile long-short portfolios with Sharpe, Sortino, drawdown, Calmar, win rate, and terminal-wealth reporting.
